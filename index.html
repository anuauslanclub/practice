ANU Auslan Club Activities

Here you can find links to pages with Auslan activities!

Practice Sentences

Sometimes it can be difficult to think of what to say! This game provides sentences of different difficulties to help you practice vocabulary and sentence structure.

Conversation Starters

This game provides a range of random questions of different difficulties to help practice vocabulary and start a conversation!

Describe the Movie

This will give you the name of a movie and you need to describe the plot to a partner so they can guess it!

Finger Twisters

Kind of like an English 'tongue twister', these sentences contain several signs that are quite similar, so practicing them can hopefully help you tell them apart!
