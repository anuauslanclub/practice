How to Use

Click the button with a speech bubble to generate a new sentence!

You can practice by yourself, or sign the sentence to a partner and get them to translate it back into English!

Easy sentences generally use simple vocabulary that you've likely already learned.

Medium sentences are a bit longer and might have a couple new signs that require you to check Signbank.

Hard sentences generally are a bit more complex and may require checking Signbank and thinking about sentence structure.

Remember to always think about sentence structure, especially when it comes to questions!
