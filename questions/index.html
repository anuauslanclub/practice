How to Use

Click the button with a speech bubble to generate a new question!

You can practice by yourself by answering, or sign the question to a partner and they can answer it!

Easy questions can be answered with one word, usually yes/no or a time (but try answering with a sentence!)

Medium questions might require one word or a couple-word answer.

Hard questions generally require a longer answer.

Remember to always think about sentence structure, especially when it comes to questions!
